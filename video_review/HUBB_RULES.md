# HUBB: eating and packaging

Product: flavoured IN-SHELL sunflower seeds.

1. Place one seed between the teeth.
2. Crack the husk with a small bite.
3. Tongue and teeth separate kernel and husk inside the mouth.
4. Bring the husk to the lips and spit it out. Keep and eat only the kernel.

Manual husk removal is an ALTERNATIVE to spitting, not another step. Husks go into the shell bag, never back into the food. Do not animate eating intact husks like chips. Internal separation is explained by narration, not visible. Tiny spit fragments are unresolved; the tutorial shows no shell bag.

Packages:
- Single sachet: 30 g; open to snack.
- Retail case: 24 × 30 g = 720 g; baqala/supermarket; sell sachets singly. Same-flavour case assumed.
- Travel cup: 5 × 30 g = 150 g; supermarket/petrol/travel; take one sealed sachet from cup.
- Family mix: 20 × 30 g = 600 g; 5 of each flavour; ecommerce/modern key accounts; share sachets.
- Shell bag: discarded husks only, not food.

Flavours: Classic Sea Salt, Garlic Salt, Pepper Lime, Fire Salt.
