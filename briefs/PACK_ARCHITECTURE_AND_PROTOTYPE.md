# HUBB Companion Pack Architecture & Prototype Brief

Version 3 · 5 September 2026 · Design-development handoff

## The idea

**A little pack for your moment. A cup for the journey. A bucket for everyone.**

HUBB is a companion to the shared pause: a passenger's road-trip snack, a parked-up scenic stop, a round between games, or a family film. The portable cup borrows the inviting, abundant feeling of a popcorn tub, while individually sealed sachets make it possible to open one portion and share the rest. A separate paper shell bag completes the ritual: enjoy the kernel, collect the shells, leave the place as you found it.

Keep the approved contemporary Saudi mural-inspired identity consistent across the range. Treat the illustration as an original cultural interpretation, not a claim of artisan manufacture, heritage certification or an award already won. The four-flavor range is fixed below; **Pepper Lime replaces Smoky BBQ throughout**.

## 1. Exact contents: the source of truth

| Format | Contents sold | Net seed contents | Pack role | Default assortment |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Individual sachet | 1 sealed 30 g sachet | 30 g | Trial, individual purchase and portion sharing | One flavor |
| Supermarket display carton | 24 individually saleable 30 g sachets | 720 g across 24 sachets | Counter or shelf display; shopper can buy one sachet | One flavor per display carton |
| Journey Cup | 5 sealed 30 g sachets + 1 folded paper shell bag | 150 g across 5 sachets | Petrol-station and convenience take-away format | All five sachets the same flavor |
| Together Bucket | 20 sealed 30 g sachets arranged as 4 groups of 5 + proposed 4 folded personal shell bags | 600 g across 20 sachets | One larger family popcorn-style bucket | 5 Classic Sea Salt + 5 Garlic Salt + 5 Pepper Lime + 5 Fire Salt |

**Counts, not loose fill, define the two cup/bucket products.** Journey Cup is not 150 g of loose seeds. Together Bucket is one outer bucket holding twenty sachets; it is not a carrier containing four Journey Cups. The four groups in the family bucket can be separated by a removable four-part divider. Four personal shell bags are a proposed inclusion for shared use, to be confirmed during costing and assembly trials.

Do not treat the 720 g supermarket carton as a new consumer portion or imply that one sachet weighs 720 g. Any mixed-flavor 24-pack, outer shipping case count or extra flavor needs a separate decision; none is specified here.

## 3. The cup-volume decision has changed

The previous 750–800 mL estimate concerned **150 g of loose in-shell seeds**. It is not an approved capacity for five finished sachets. This architecture introduces filled sachet shapes, seals, film, trapped air, packing gaps and a folded shell bag. A cup size cannot be selected reliably from seed weight alone.

**No manufacturing dimensions are approved in this document.** The Journey Cup must be sized around five production-representative filled sachets. The Together Bucket must be sized around twenty such sachets, its dividers and the selected number of folded bags. Rendered proportions are visual intentions only.

### Supplier packing trial

1. Produce representative 30 g filled and sealed sachets in the proposed film and seal format. Use the actual finished seeds for each of the four flavors, not empty film or loose-volume estimates. Record filled width, height, thickness, seal widths and how much variation is observed.
2. Mock up at least two sachet geometries. Compare a slimmer sachet and a shorter/wider sachet for filling, one-hand retrieval, label readability and packing into a tapered cup. Do not choose a geometry solely because it looks slim in a render.
3. For the Journey Cup, compare a five-pack fan with an upright bundle. Add the folded shell bag in a separate dry sleeve. Test five actual sachets per mock-up, including the lid, rim and any internal paper retention band.
4. Choose the smallest practical cup that closes without forcing, bending the seals or requiring the consumer to pull hard on sachets. Keep useful finger access. Record the internal top diameter, base diameter, usable height and actual assembled outside dimensions.
5. For the Together Bucket, test a removable four-way divider with five sachets per compartment. Confirm twenty sachets fit beneath a closed lid with the proposed four folded bags. Test extraction from the last, partly empty compartment as well as the full bucket.
6. Perform a bench-use trial: open the outer pack, remove the sachets, open one sachet, pour it into the empty cup or a separate serving bowl, set up the shell bag and return the unopened sachets to their retention sleeve. Observe which steps need clearer instructions.
7. Ask the converter to assess the final materials, food-contact surfaces, closures, sealing, abrasion, handling and transport suitability against the intended product and distribution conditions. The converter supplies the final dielines and print specifications.
8. Approve a filled physical sample, not only a digital render. Record final dimensions, component count, assembly sequence and unit cost before freezing artwork.

For road-trip imagery, place the cup on a stable passenger-side surface or in a holder tested with the actual pack. **Do not claim universal vehicle cup-holder fit.** The larger family bucket is for shared stops, picnics, home and group occasions; it is not a console cup.

## 4. Consumer ritual: simple and physically honest

### Journey Cup: five sealed packs, one at a time

1. **Open:** remove the lid and lift out the five sealed sachets and folded shell bag.
2. **Choose:** open one 30 g sachet. Keep the other four sealed in the proposed removable paper band/sleeve or an appropriate bag. The retention component is a prototype proposal, not a proven feature.
3. **Enjoy:** pour the opened sachet into the now-empty Journey Cup, or take seeds directly from the opened sachet. The cup's inside serving surface must be appropriate for this intended use.
4. **Collect:** unfold the paper shell bag and place it upright in a stable side holder. Collect the empty shells there. Do not mix shell waste with unopened food or use the food cup for shell waste while it is serving seeds.
5. **Finish:** fold the shell bag closed and put it in a suitable bin. Keep the remaining sachets sealed for later or give them to companions.

This is an open-one-pack ritual, not an instruction to decant all five sachets at once. For a photographed travel moment, use a parked vehicle or a passenger setting; no scene should show a driver cracking seeds or reaching across driving controls.

### Together Bucket: everyone chooses

Open the one bucket, choose among the four flavor compartments and pass individual sachets to the group. Set up one proposed personal shell bag per sharing position. Keep the unopened sachets in their compartments. For serving loose seeds, use separate clean bowls; do not combine food and used shells in the family bucket.

### Paper shell bag

Develop a small, freestanding gusseted paper bag with an opening that is easy to use without touching shared food. Its finished size, construction and moisture tolerance must be checked by the supplier with the intended use. A paper bag is not assumed to be leakproof, reusable or suitable for storing food simply because it looks like kraft paper.

Store the clean folded bag separately from loose seeds; in this architecture the seed sachets remain sealed until use. In the cup, use a separate dry sleeve or a clearly identified accessory space. In the family bucket, provide a designated accessory pocket rather than mixing bags between open serving compartments.

**Draft front copy:**

- Arabic: للقشور · خلّ المكان أجمل
- English: SHELLS HERE · LEAVE THE MOMENT CLEAN

**Draft small instruction:** Fold closed after use. Place in a suitable bin.

Use a small shell-to-bag pictogram, not a mouth, spitting face or graphic saliva image. Keep the bag upright; never depict it attached to a steering wheel, gear selector, airbag area or other driving control. Native Arabic copy approval remains part of the artwork handoff.

## 7. Visual realism and count QA

The annotated pack architecture above is the authoritative count reference. Photorealistic AI imagery can make hidden contents look plausible without proving the exact count; do not use a closed-pack render as count verification.

- **Hero Journey Cup:** one cup, exactly five matching-flavor sachets when all contents are displayed, one shell bag. If one sachet is open, show four still sealed plus the opened one, not a sixth packet.
- **Hero Together Bucket:** one bucket, four compartments, five sachets per compartment when shown in a count-demonstration view; twenty sachets total. Proposed four personal shell bags should appear as a separate group.
- **Retail display demonstration:** exactly 24 sachets per full display. Prefer an explanatory top-down 4 × 6 arrangement or a separate annotated diagram for count proof; a perspective row of partly hidden sachets is not sufficient.
- **Flavor range:** Classic Sea Salt / Garlic Salt / Pepper Lime / Fire Salt, no Smoky BBQ and no accidental fifth flavor.
- **Contents distinction:** unopened sachets must not visually dissolve into a pile of loose seeds inside the closed cup. If seeds are served loose, show the opened sachet and explain the decanting step.
- **Human treatment:** realistic adult hands, sleeves or shoulders only; no faces, no mouths, no spitting, no hands holding seeds over a steering wheel. Use parked-up travel, passenger-side handling, group tables and between-round gaming scenes.
- **Seed truth:** in-shell sunflower seeds with believable scale and cracks; no candy kernels, giant single seeds, implausibly large cutaways or a shell bag visibly used as a serving-food container.
- **Design consistency:** same logo, same flavor colors and the same base sachet geometry in the cup, family bucket, display and e-commerce scene. A single sachet must not change shape merely to fit each render.
- **Claims:** a crack/inside-flavor concept can be expressed visually, but do not put an unverified absolute such as “perfect crack every time” or “flavor through every kernel” into final production artwork.

## 8. What is ready, and what makes it real

Ready for creative development: the four flavors, exact pack counts, format roles, sharing story, content hierarchy and no-face scene direction.

Required to freeze production artwork: a production-representative 30 g sachet; the approved cup/bucket/display/bag sample; supplier dielines; confirmed materials and closures; measured print colors; confirmed accessory bill of materials; approved Arabic; final factual product copy and retail codes.

The immediate next physical step is **one five-sachet cup trial and one twenty-sachet family-bucket trial using actual filled sachets**. Their measurements replace all concept dimensions. This brief proposes the structure and use experience; it does not certify manufacturing performance or regulatory compliance.
