# HUBB — Bring HUBB along

Saudi companion-snack story and proposed eight-week pilot · 5 September 2026

Working brief: timing, outlets and targets are proposed assumptions. No budget, distribution or commercial agreements are confirmed.

## 1. The emotional idea

**حُبّ — للّمة طعم**  
**HUBB — Togetherness has a taste.**

Hero invitation: **خذ حُبّ معك** — **Bring HUBB along.**

Some moments need no big plan. A road break. One more round. Everyone reaching for their favourite. HUBB brings sunflower-seed flavours in little packs made to pass around—with a place for the shells, too. Open one now. Keep the others sealed. The cup goes with the outing; the big bucket brings everyone together.

HUBB should become the thing someone remembers to bring. Individual sachets, easy sharing and shell disposal make companionship tangible. Popcorn supplies the sharing format, not a cinema affiliation.

Arabic here is creative draft for Saudi-language review. Do not claim an award, guaranteed virality, superior kernel flavour or a perfect crack without evidence.

## 2. Culture that explains the product

The approved Asiri-inspired direction uses white ground, geometric mural bands and four flavour colours. UNESCO describes Al-Qatt Al-Asiri as associated with Asir's guest rooms and collaborative, intergenerational making. **Welcoming people and sharing** is the creative connection—not a claim that this concept is authentic artisan artwork. [UNESCO](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/al-qatt-al-asiri-female-traditional-interior-wall-decoration-in-asir-saudi-arabia-01261)

Commission and credit a Saudi/Asiri artist for production patterns. Connect their rhythm across four flavours so the family bucket feels like one gathering.

## 3. The pack is the promise

| Format | Exact product architecture | Shopper message | Primary role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual sachet | 1 × 30 g, one flavour | “Pick your flavour.” | Accessible individual purchase |
| Retail display | 24 × 30 g, same flavour; proposed assumption | “Take one.” | Open-front, refillable-facing display; singles sold individually |
| Companion cup | 5 sealed 30 g sachets, same flavour; 150 g total; one folded paper shell bag | “Five little packs. Ready to go.” | Petrol convenience and outings |
| Family bucket | 20 sealed 30 g sachets: five of each of four flavours; 600 g total; four personal shell bags proposed | “Four flavours. One gathering.” | Sharing, gaming nights and planned grocery baskets |

Four flavours: **Classic Sea Salt / Garlic Salt / Pepper Lime / Fire Salt**. Cup: sachets, not loose seeds. Family: flavour-grouped sachets, not nested cups. Test volume with filled sachets; the former loose-seed estimate does not size this format.

Front hierarchy: HUBB first, flavour second, pack count and net weight in a small consistent footer—never oversized. Show a discreet shell-bag icon and **كيس للقشور / SHELL BAG**. Keep shell disposal separate from unopened food. Four family-pack bags give people their own disposal point.

## 4. Audience and channel roles

Audience: adult friends and families at outings and parked road breaks; passengers, gaming groups and hosts. Motivation: flavour choice, portable portions and less mess.

Saudi domestic tourism is substantial: DataSaudi reports 93.3 million domestic tourists in 2025. This supports investigating travel occasions, but it does **not** establish road-trip share, unique customer counts or HUBB demand. [DataSaudi](https://datasaudi.sa/en/sector/tourism)

| Proposed channel | Format and placement | Content and commercial task |
|---|---|---|
| Petrol convenience | Cup near the indoor snack checkout; singles beside it | “Bring HUBB along”; demonstrate five sachets plus bag. Begin with an agreed pilot, not a national placement claim. Medium effort. |
| Supermarket | Four separate 24-sachet displays in the snack aisle or approved checkout location | Large flavour label, visible individual price ticket and tear-front display. Medium effort. |
| Hypermarket | Family bucket in snacks; cup adjacent if space agreed | Show the full 20-sachet contents and four-flavour split. A temporary endcap is a proposed placement. High effort. |
| Ninja / HungerStation | Cup for small gatherings, family bucket for planned evenings | Clean thumbnail, pack-count title, contents image, stock accuracy. Medium effort after onboarding. |
| Amazon.sa | Family bucket; cup if economics support shipping | Searchable bilingual detail pages, exact counts, dimensions, ingredient and pack information. Medium effort. |
| HUBB website / owned social | One brand destination; choose outing, gaming or sharing | Explain the ritual, compare formats, show confirmed buying options only. Medium effort. |

SASCO's 2025 annual report describes Palm stores selling snacks and on-the-go essentials to motorists: channel evidence, not a partnership. [SASCO report, pp. 52–53](https://www.saudiexchange.sa/Resources/fsPdf/11601_441_2026-05-20_10-44-14_en.pdf)

## 5. Website and no-face creative system

Homepage order: **emotional hero → cup unpacking → choose your moment → four flavours → family bucket contents → shell-bag ritual → confirmed availability**. Keep the product category “roasted in-shell sunflower seeds” clear; this is not popcorn.

Must-have assets: four individual sachet packshots; four cup packshots; cup contents demonstration with exactly five sealed sachets; 20-sachet family contents view; 24-single retail display; petrol counter scene; supermarket shelf; hypermarket family display; product-detail images; three lifestyle stories.

Lifestyle stories, all face-free and realistic:

- **Road break:** stationary unbranded vehicle, passenger-side view, hands passing a sachet, cup secure on a flat surface and open shell bag nearby. No driver eating while controlling a moving car.
- **Between rounds:** controllers resting on the table, adult hands opening separate sachets, drinks and soft screen glow. No borrowed game logos.
- **Everyone's favourite:** overhead family-table composition, four colours circulating, 20 sachets emerging from one bucket, individual shell bags. No shared spitting scene.

Leave clean image space for live Arabic text rather than relying on generated text. Every retail or platform mockup should be identified as a proposed placement. Exact package counts matter more than decorative abundance.

## 6. Eight-week pilot

Proposed scope: one city, ten permissioned outlets—six petrol-convenience and four grocery—and one onboarded digital route. Establish product, trade, delivery and creative costs before paid media.

| Week | Deliverable / action | Owner and dependency |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Test cup/bucket fit with real sachets; interview 15 adult shoppers about occasion and pack-count clarity | Product + research; physical samples |
| 2 | Approve Arabic, artist direction, pack labels and unit economics; recruit pilot stores | Brand + operations + sales |
| 3 | Produce no-face stills and three short ritual films; build bilingual product pages | Creative + web; approved packs |
| 4 | Install displays; train store staff; record opening stock and shelf photographs | Trade; written outlet acceptance |
| 5 | Test road-break versus gaming creative with the same offer and comparable delivery | Growth; active stock and permitted tracking |
| 6 | Test family contents-first versus emotional-table imagery; collect 30 voluntary responses | Growth + research; keep format and price consistent |
| 7 | Review sell-through, shelf availability, damage and reorder behaviour; correct replenishment | Sales + operations |
| 8 | Decide expand, revise or pause by format and outlet; document what changed | Founder + growth; verified sales and costs |

## 7. “Algorithm-based” means a learning loop

**Creative → voluntary occasion choice → format selection → purchase, where measurable → stock/reorder → next creative and replenishment decision.**

Track creative ID, selected occasion, flavour, format, channel, outlet and date. Use aggregate counts and consented analytics; no facial recognition, precise-travel surveillance, inferred sensitive traits or compulsory account for flavour voting. Retail stock reports can improve decisions without identifying shoppers.

Proposed pilot decision targets—not forecasts or industry benchmarks:

- Primary: at least **6 of 10 participating outlets reorder** once by week eight, without forced stock loading.
- Availability: at least **90% of scheduled outlet checks** find the agreed range available.
- Comprehension: **24 of 30 surveyed adults** correctly identify five sachets in the cup and 20 in the family bucket after viewing the contents image.
- Track units sold per outlet-week, by format; report stockouts separately. A 24-unit case shipment is not 24 consumer sales.
- Track digital conversion and contribution after product cost, packaging, trade/platform fees, fulfilment and media. Set acceptable acquisition cost only after those inputs exist.

Review weekly. Small creative differences are not proven winners. Expand where supply, understanding, repeat purchase and contribution support it.
