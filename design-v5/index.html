01 / AN ARTISTIC SAUDI EXPRESSION

A little artistry.
A lot of belonging.

Warm ivory makes space for a generous Arabic gesture, a sweep of flavour and the familiar pleasure of a seed worth cracking.

The Saudi connection lives in the gathering: making space, passing something around, staying for another story. The pack carries that feeling through calligraphy and a delicate woven rhythm.

The visual story is shared across every flavour and format. Change the flavour colour; keep the soul of the pack.

↳

Saudi pride, expressed through
the way we come together.
